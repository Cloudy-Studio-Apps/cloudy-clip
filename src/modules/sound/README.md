# sound

The Sound page, a tab between Edit and Export, and the mix of a composition: how each audio track and the master sound, with two mixes to switch between. The page has a transport row, the audio-only timeline over a panel (Clip, Channel and Loudness) and a small preview on the left, and the mixer on the right with Master pinned at its edge (layout B, `docs/prototype/designs/sound-page.html`). The values live in `project` (`Track.mix`, `Composition.mix`), since core modules read them; this module owns the page, the commands and the tools.

- **Tier:** feature
- **Requires:** `project`, `timeline` (the panel, `setTrackMuted`, `splitAt`, Snap), `preview` (the stage, play, the timecode, live mix, solo, monitor, meters, loudness, `dynamicsTransfer`), `inspector` (`ClipTabSections` in the Clip tab; `InspectorSection` and `FieldRow` in the Channel tab), `audio` (`EqEditor`, `SoundRow`), `settings` (the Sound presets section), `media` (noise reduction's rendered files, `getProbe`; `prepareFile` to read a take before `media.import` places it), `workspace` (the project's folder for a take, `beforeLeave`)
- **Contributes:** timeline overlay `sound.recordingTake` (`timelineOverlays`, scope `sound`: the take growing on its lane, or on a dashed lane of its own under the last track while it goes onto a new Voice track, `ui/lanes/RecordingTake.tsx`); page `sound.page` (`SOUND_PAGE`; `appPages`, icon `AudioLines`, `keepsPlayback: true`, `timelineScope: "sound"`: playback goes on and Space, S and Delete work here, and the timeline edits sound only); section `sound.presets` (`settingsSections`, "Sound presets", `ui/settings/PresetsSection.tsx`)
- **Session tools on the page:** markers (`marker`: flags, snapping and the M keys reach the Sound timeline), the loop (Loop in the transport, handles on the ruler) and audio scrub (dragging on the ruler plays slices of sound through the mix), and recording a voice onto a track (below).
- **Points it owns:** —
- **API** (`SoundApi`, per kernel): `view` (`SoundViewState`: `selected` (a track id, `"master"` or null), `solo`, `expanded`, `monitor`, `armed` (the track a take goes onto); never in history), `select(target | null)`, `setExpanded(expanded)`, `arm(trackId | null)` (refused while a take is under way), `recorder` (`Recorder`: `state` (`status`: `idle`, `starting`, `recording`, `stopping`; `trackId`, null during a take onto a new Voice track until it's placed; `at`), `start(trackId | null)` (null: onto a new Voice track), `stop({ pause? })` (returns the stop under way, if any), `arm(trackId | null)`, `level()` and `length()`, read per frame), `presets` (the user's own, `UserPresets`). The commands are exported from `index.ts`.
- **Files:**
  - `sound.mix.commands.ts`: the mix commands below. Inputs are built from the schemas in `project` (`volumeDbSchema`, `panSchema`). `sound.guards.ts`: `audioTrack` (the audio track or `sound.track_not_found` / `sound.not_audio`), shared with the listening commands.
  - `sound.effects.commands.ts`: the effect-chain commands below, on an audio track or `"master"`. Every one reads the chain, edits a copy and writes it in one step through `sound.mix.write.ts` (`writeTrack`, `writeComposition`, `targetComposition`, shared with the mix commands), so an empty chain stores nothing. `setEffect` merges the patch into the effect and parses it with the kind's schema (`effectSchemas` in `project`), so out-of-range values and unknown fields are refused. `logic/effects.ts`: `defaultSpec(kind)` (typed by kind), `chainWhere(target, compositionId)` (the `{ target, compositionId? }` input every chain command from the UI takes), `closesLoop(tracks, owner, listensTo)` (walks the listens-to graph; self counts). `logic/effects.ts` also has `EFFECT_NAMES` (full and short), `addableKinds(chain, target)` (not there yet; no duck on master), `chainOf(state, target, compositionId)` (the stored array, one shared empty one, so selectors are stable) `placesUnheardEffects(state, trackId)`. `logic/denoise.ts`: `denoiseRequests(needs, hasSound)` (the renderings `project`'s `denoiseNeeds` calls for, files with sound only: the forward rendering for every need, which export mixes from, then the reversed copy for reversed clips, which the preview plays). `logic/chain.ts`: `chainSlot` (where a dragged row lands). `logic/presets.ts`: `BUILT_IN_PRESETS` (Clear voice, Podcast, Phone, Radio, Warm) as plain data.
  - `sound.denoise.commands.ts`: `sound.measureFloor` reads the noise floor of a second (or `duration`) of a track at `at` (default the actor's playhead) through `sound.service.ts` (`measureNoiseFloor`, the native `sound_measure_floor` in `src-tauri/src/sound/`: the overall noise floor `astats` reads over a stretch of the original, rounded, within what `afftdn` accepts, -80 to -20 dBFS) and sets the floor of the track's noise reduction with `sound.setEffect`, one undo step. Noise reduction itself is rendered per file and settings, which the preview plays and the export mixes from; `project`'s `heardDenoise` and `denoiseNeeds` say which clips get it: the top-level track's own, otherwise the master's (per file, for the tracks without their own; not a bus effect). It always applies first, before clip volume and the rest of the chain, wherever it sits in the chain. `sound.denoise.follow.ts` (`followDenoiseNeeds`, from `setup`): 800 ms after the document or the probes last change, every file `denoiseNeeds` lists in any composition is wanted (`media.wantDenoised`) and only those, so a run of nudges renders the last setting and queued ones left behind are dropped.
  - `sound.presets.commands.ts`: `sound.savePreset`, `sound.renamePreset`, `sound.removePreset` on the user's own presets. `sound.presets.store.ts`: `createUserPresets()`, `core`'s `createSavedStore` under `localStorage` key `cloudy-clip.sound-presets` (each preset parsed on its own, its effects with their kind's schema from `project` without the id; one that doesn't parse is dropped, never the whole list). Never in a project: applying one writes plain values. A saved duck listens to no track (tracks belong to one project). No MCP tools: agents list the user's presets (`sound_list_presets`, `own: true`) and apply them, never change them.
  - `sound.monitor.commands.ts`: `sound.setSolo`, `sound.setMonitor` and `sound.clearListening`. They write `view` and push the result to `preview.sound` (`setSolo`, `setMonitor`). Not undoable and no MCP tools: they act on the user's ears, like `settings.open`.
  - `sound.leave.ts`: `followSoundPageLeave(pages, onLeave)`. The module runs `sound.clearListening` each time the user leaves the Sound page: solo, Mono and Dim show nowhere else, so Edit never plays soloed or dimmed without saying so. The speaker volume stays, like a knob on the desk.
  - **Recording** (Linux; see `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-microphone-check.md`). Where it is offered is one constant, `RECORDING_CHECKED` in `logic/record.ts` (`linux: true`, `windows: false` until the owner's WebView2 check passes, `macos: false` until the bundle has `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription`, `android: false`); elsewhere Record and the arm buttons are absent (`canRecordHere`, in `sound.record.capture.ts`). R on a strip (`ui/lanes/ArmButton.tsx`, a `StripToggle`) arms an audio track that isn't muted (`canArm`; a muted track isn't offered; groups, when they come, won't be either); `recorder.arm` refuses while a take is under way. **Record records onto the armed track, or onto a new Voice track when none is armed.** Record in the transport (`ui/transport/RecordButton.tsx`, enabled whenever a composition is open) runs `recorder.start` with the armed track when it can take the take (an audio track that isn't muted, in the open composition), otherwise with null: the take then goes onto a new audio track at the bottom of the open composition, named `Voice 1`, `Voice 2`… (`VOICE_TRACK` in `logic/record.ts`, numbered by `timeline`'s `nextTrackName`), made by `sound.placeRecording` in the take's own undo step, so one undo removes the take and its track together, and a take with nothing kept, or that fails, leaves no track behind. Until then `RecordingTake` draws it on a dashed lane under the last track, named as the track will be (the mixer has no strip for it yet, so no input meter); once placed, the new track is armed, so the next take goes there too. While the microphone opens the button reads "Starting…"; while it records it's a red Stop (`--mark`, `--mark-foreground`) with a pulsing mark (`ui/transport/RecordDot.tsx`, a small square, in `--mark` on Record) and how long the take has run, `mm:ss` (`ui/transport/RecordingClock.tsx`: read from `recorder.length()` each frame, written through the DOM only when the second changes); while the take is written and placed, "Saving…". `recorder.start`: `sound.record.capture.ts` (`openCapture`) asks for the microphone with echo cancellation, noise suppression and gain control off, and runs it through its own `AudioContext` into `sound.record.worklet.ts` (bundled with `?worker&url` and loaded with `audioWorklet.addModule`, as the preview's loudness and dynamics worklets are), which keeps nothing until `keep()` (sent right after playback starts, so the take starts with the next block) and posts the first channel in batches of 512 samples; stopping flushes the last partial batch before the microphone closes. The take is recorded at that context's rate. **Where takes go:** `Recordings/` in the project's folder; for a project with no file yet, `Recordings/` in the workspace's `folder()` (Documents/Cloudy Clip), where that project will be saved, so the take ends up beside it, never in a cache. `sound.service.ts`: `beginTake` (`sound_record_begin`: `Recording <date> <time>.wav`, ` 2`, ` 3`… when taken, never overwritten; the project's folder must exist, or at least its parent; a take a reloaded page left open is closed first) and `writeTake` (`sound_record_write`: 16-bit samples as a raw body, a quarter of a second at a time, at most 1 MB a chunk; the native side rewrites the WAV header after every chunk, so the file is whole even if the app stops; `done` closes it, and a take with nothing in it is deleted). Then `preview.play`, and the take starts at the playhead where playback started (`at`). It runs on past the composition's end. While it records the loop is off (playback runs on through its end; it's set again after the take, if its composition is still open) and audio scrub is silenced (`preview.holdScrub`), so the open microphone never hears a slice. While it records, the armed strip's meters show the microphone (`Meter` target `"input"`, from `recorder.level()` through the page's meter loop) and `RecordingTake` draws the take on its lane each frame through `style` until it's placed; the document doesn't change until then. Stop (the button, a pause, playing again after the end, any jump of the playhead by more than a frame from where the time passed puts it (a ruler click or drag, Home, a marker, a seek from anywhere: the follower watches the user's clock, so no caller checks), leaving the Sound page, opening another composition: `sound.record.follow.ts`; what was recorded up to then is placed; the project about to be left, through the workspace's `beforeLeave`) pauses, flushes and closes the file, reads it outside the bus (`media.prepareFile`) and runs `sound.placeRecording` as its own undoable step. `stop()` during a stop returns the one under way, so window close waits for the placement before saving. `workspace.new` and `workspace.open` refuse with `workspace.recording` while a take is under way. What can't be placed, or a write that failed (a full disk), is told with the file's path; a take is never lost silently. `sound.record.ts` (`createRecorder`), `logic/record.ts` (`toPcm16`, `joinChunks`, `takeStem`, `folderOf`, `startFailure`, `platformOf`, `formatElapsed`, `VOICE_TRACK`). Native side: `src-tauri/src/sound/record.rs` (`Takes`), `microphone.rs` (`permission_request`, set on the builder in `lib.rs`: the microphone is allowed for the app's own pages only, the dev server's `http://localhost:1420` in debug builds only; WebKitGTK denies it otherwise, with no prompt). No MCP tool: the microphone is the user's.

- **Invariants:** a mix equal to the default is never stored. Only audio tracks have a mix. Switching A and B swaps the live values (every audio track's mix, and the master) with `otherMix`; the first time, B starts as a copy of A, and tracks deleted since are left out. A switch is one undo step and swaps the effect chains with the volumes. A chain holds one effect of each kind; a duck never listens to itself or into a loop, the master cannot duck (`sound.duck_on_master`, in every command that writes a chain), and a deleted track leaves every `listensTo` in the step that deletes it (`project`'s `settle`). `sound.resetTrackMix` keeps the effects. Solo and the monitor live in this module's `view`, per kernel, and never reach the document, history or an export. Solo, Mono and Dim last only while the Sound page is open.
