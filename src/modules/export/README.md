# export

Turns a composition into a file. The Export page, the last tab of the top bar, sets what to make; exports wait in a queue and run one at a time while you keep editing.

The settings sections fold one by one, and all open again when you come back to the page.

- **Tier:** feature
- **Requires:** `project`, `media`, `preview`, `workspace` (the default file name)
- **Contributes:** page `export.page` (`appPages`, group `PAGE_GROUP_DELIVER`, icon `Upload`)
- **API** (`useModule(exportRef)` / `ctx.require(exportRef)`): `queue`, `form`, `capabilities`, `runner`
- **Files:**
  - Speed: frames come from the preview engine, which follows `sourceTimeAt`. Sound carries `speed`, `keepPitch` and `reversed` to `src-tauri/src/export/recipe.rs`, which reads `duration * speed` of the file, and `soundfilters.rs` adds `areverse`, then `atempo` (or `asetrate` when `keepPitch` is false) before the clip's own settings. Reversed clips get a full-quality copy before rendering (`media.prepareReversed`). A ramped clip's sound starts at `sourceTimeAt(clip, start)` and carries `tempoSteps` instead of `speed`: one for each stretch where the rate holds (before the first key, after the last, on a Hold or between equal keys) and one per 250 ms of timeline time where it changes (rounded, the last one taking what is left, none shorter than 50 ms unless the segment is, so their lengths add up to the segment's), each with its `from` in the file and the `speed` that plays its part of the file in its time. `soundfilters.rs` splits the input (`asplit`); each step reads its part with `atrim` (in samples, 100 ms further, since `atempo` gives a few milliseconds more or less), `asetpts`, its tempo (or `asetrate` when `keepPitch` is false), and is cut to its exact length (`apad`, `atrim`), every step but the last 5 ms longer. Neighbours are joined by `acrossfade` over those 5 ms with `hsin` (gains that add up to 1, as both sides hold nearly the same sound), one pair at a time (more inputs need FFmpeg 7.1), so the next step fades in from its own start: no jump at a boundary, each step stays on the picture and the segment keeps its length. All of this comes before the clip's own settings, envelopes and fades. Labels carry the segment's number and the step's.
  - `export.commands.ts`: `enqueue` checks everything and keeps the document as it is, so later edits don't reach a queued export.
  - `export.runner.ts`: one job at a time, outside the bus. A file with a proxy gets its master first (`media.prepareMaster`), the webview's video levels are measured once (`preview.measureVideoLevels`), then each frame is drawn by `preview.createRenderer` and posted to FFmpeg, which sets the pace.
  - `logic/mixdown.ts`: what sounds in a stretch of a composition, at any depth, as stretches of the original files, with their speed or tempo steps, and how the tracks they sit on mix. It returns `{ segments, mix }`. Muted tracks and disabled clips are left out; clips under a junction get equal-power fades.
  - The mix tree: each `SoundSegment` has `track`, an index into `ExportSpec.mix.tracks` (the composition's audio tracks in order, each `{ gain, pan }` as a linear level) beside `mix.master` (`{ gain }`). A clip sounds through its top-level track; inside a placed composition it takes the outer track's index and the inner tracks' volume and pan are folded into its own gain and pan by `soundInPlacement` (`project`), the function the preview's `placeSound` uses, so both sides fold alike. Solo is never here: it isn't in the document. `src-tauri/src/export/mixgraph.rs` builds the tree as the preview's buses: the clips of a track are summed (`amix`), the track's `volume` and `soundfilters::pan_filter` apply to that sum, the tracks are summed, the master's `volume` follows, then `apad,atrim` to `[mixed]`. A track at the defaults adds no bus and no filter (its clips go straight to the last sum, which equals summing them first), so a default mix is one `amix` of every clip, as before.
  - Effects in the mix: `TrackMixSpec.effects` and `mix.master.effects` carry the chains that change the sound as `MixEffect` (`export.types.ts`): off effects, noise reduction (a rendered file, below) and an EQ without a working band are left out, and a duck's `listensTo` becomes indexes into `mix.tracks` (a track not there is dropped; a muted one keeps its index but has no clips, so it keys nothing). Effects of the tracks inside a placed composition are not applied, as in the preview. `src-tauri/src/export/mixeffects.rs` turns each into the filter the preview's ports match sample for sample (the table in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-sound-dynamics-check.md`): `acompressor`/`agate`/`sidechaincompress` with `detection=peak`, `deesser=m=0.5:f=√(1−e^(−2π·Hz/22050))`, `alimiter=attack=5:level=disabled` (latency off), the duck at `threshold=-40dB:ratio=20:mix=1−10^(depth/20)`; the EQ effect reuses `soundfilters::eq_filters`, the clip EQ's. The order is the preview bus's: a track is its clips summed → its effects in order → pan → level; the master is the tracks summed → its level → its effects → `apad,atrim`. A duck's key is the tracks it listens to after their levels: each listened track ends in `asplit` (`[t<n>]` for the master, `[k<n>_<listener>]` for each duck), the key is their `amix` (`normalize=0`), and the ducked track goes through `sidechaincompress`. Tracks are built listened-first (a loop, which the document refuses, drops the key of its first track). A duck whose tracks have no sound in the range is left out (a silent key lowers nothing), and a track with no sound keys or splits nothing. A chain with effects, and the key, are padded in silence to the export's length plus 100 ms, so the limiter gives back its lookahead and the duck never ends waiting for its key, as the preview's effects run on; the last `atrim` cuts it. A duck on the master is ignored (refused by the document). Checked end to end against the preview's runner: see the check doc.
  - Noise reduction: a clip heard through it (`project`'s `heardDenoise`, the rule `denoiseNeeds` and the preview follow: its top-level track's, otherwise the master's; inside a placement, the outer track's; a reversed clip's WAV gets `areverse` like any reversed sound) is mixed from its file rendered with those settings (`media.denoised`), so the segment's `path` is that WAV and `mixeffects.rs` has no filter for it. `mixdown` takes the file through `soundFile`; `export.guards.ts` (`denoisedFiles`, `checkDenoised`) asks for each, which starts a rendering that hadn't, and `export.enqueue` fails with `export.denoise_pending` while any is still rendering.
  - Keyed volume and pan: `logic/envelope.ts` samples a value at each key's timeline time (loop and ping-pong repeats included, and 1 ms before each, so a Hold step stays a step) and every 100 ms, with no cap, and drops the points on the straight line between their neighbours as `gainEnvelope` / `panEnvelope`, `at` counted from the segment's start, read with `trackValue(track, contentTime(clip, time))`. They replace `gain` and `pan`; the parent placement's gain and a crossfade still multiply in. `soundfilters.rs` turns them into `volume=eval=frame` expressions of `t` (`gain_envelope_filter`, `pan_envelope_filter`): gain where the gain goes, and a pan through four gains (`pan_gains`, the law of the still `pan_filter`) between `channelsplit` and `join`, before the fades. Expressions are balanced trees of `if` (FFmpeg stops parsing at 100 levels of nesting), constant stretches collapse. `recipe.rs` puts the segments' chains together with `mixgraph.rs`'s tree. A graph over 8 KB goes in `<partial>.filter` (Windows allows about 32 KB for a whole command line) and is read with `-/filter_complex` (FFmpeg 7+) or `-filter_complex_script` (older; `ffmpeg -version` decides, asked once off the async runtime's thread and kept for the app's lifetime: `job::script_option`); the file is removed on success, failure, cancel and by the start-up sweep.
  - `logic/timing.ts`: a frame is drawn 1 ms into its time. A time rounded to microseconds can fall just before its frame, and a seek there lands on the previous one.
  - `logic/presets.ts`: kinds, formats and the 10–100 quality scale as encoder numbers. `logic/estimate.ts`: rough size and time.
  - `export.guards.ts`: what `enqueue` refuses, and where the file goes.
  - `export.service.ts`: the native calls. `ui/`: the page.
  - Native (`src-tauri/src/export`): `recipe.rs` turns a structured spec into FFmpeg arguments and builds each segment's chain from `soundfilters.rs` (tempo, pitch, equalizer, pan, envelopes), `mixgraph.rs` joins the chains in the track and master tree, `job.rs` runs one FFmpeg on `<name>.partial.<ext>` and renames it when complete, `frames.rs` takes frames as raw RGBA over loopback HTTP (a frame is too big for the JSON path IPC takes on Linux), `encoders.rs` says what this FFmpeg can write and which graphics card encoder really works. Quitting doesn't stop a running FFmpeg by itself: it ends when its input closes, and the partial file left by a quit or crash is removed on the next launch.
- **Facts worth knowing:** about 13 frames a second at 1080p on the development machine. Size and frame rate come from the composition. FFmpeg is the system binary; without it the page says how to install it.
- **Not yet:** VAAPI, pausing a job, exporting several compositions in one action.
