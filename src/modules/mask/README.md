# mask

Masks on picture clips and track mattes. A mask is a shape (rectangle, ellipse, star, heart, linear, mirror, or a drawn path) in the clip's picture that decides where it shows, feathered, grown or shrunk, combined with the other masks by mode. A track matte shows a clip only where another video track's picture is. Like After Effects' masks and track mattes.

- **Tier:** feature
- **Requires:** `project` (the document, `orderedMasks`, `animatedClip`, `writeKey`, `maskOutline`, `insertPoint`), `element` (which kinds draw a picture), `preview` (the actor's playhead, the default `at` of `mask_list`), `keyframe` (`keyTargets`)
- **Contributes:** key target `mask.keys` (`keyTargets`, `mask.keys.ts`): every keyable number of a video clip's masks, one group per mask (`prefix` `masks.<id>.`, the mask's name); point fields are labeled "Point <n> · X" (n from 1, in order). When the last key goes, `commitStatic` writes the value through `mask.update` (or `mask.setPoints` for a point)
- **Points it owns:** —
- **API** (`useModule(maskRef)`): `ui` (`MaskUiState`, per kernel, never in history: `selectedMaskId`, `selectedPointId`, `drawing`)
- **Files:**
  - `mask.commands.ts`: add, update, remove, reorder. `mask.matte.commands.ts`: `setMatte`. `mask.build.ts`: a new mask with the defaults. `mask.guards.ts`: `requirePictureClip`, `requireMask`, `checkRange`. `mask.schema.ts`: the Zod inputs.
  - `mask.points.commands.ts`: `setPoints`, `convert`. `logic/keys.ts`: `pathKeyTimes` (content times of a path's point and scale keys), sampling points and preset shapes at a content time, a preset's outline as points (`outlinePoints`, segments of no length at every time dropped by `dropFlat`), `keyInto`.
  - `mask.mcp.ts`: the tools, and `mask_list`. `mask.store.ts`: `createMaskUi()`.
- **Data:** `clip.masks`, `clip.maskOrder` and `clip.matte` live in the document (`project` declares and validates them, see its README). Mask space is output px from the picture's center, the picture fitted in the frame before the clip's transform. Key paths (all shared numbers, with `MASK_RANGES`): `masks.<id>.opacity | feather | expansion | x | y | rotation`, `masks.<id>.shape.width | height | roundness | points | inner | scale` (those its kind has) and per point `masks.<id>.shape.points.<pid>.x | y | inX | inY | outX | outY` (relative to the mask's x/y, before `scale`).
- **Invariants:** only picture clips on video tracks have masks or a matte. Removing a mask drops its keys; removing the last drops `masks` and `maskOrder`. A matte names another video track of the clip's composition; a matte track has no matte of its own, and a clip on a matte track has none (`mask.matte_chain`). Every command is one undo step.
- **Animated paths:** when any point coordinate has keys, `mask.setPoints` with `at` keys the six coordinates of each changed point there (other points untouched); a point inserted with `insertAt` gets keys at every `pathKeyTimes` time, placed by `insertPoint` (de Casteljau) on that time's shape at the same `t`, and its neighbors' facing handles are keyed to match, so every keyed shape stays the same. A removed point's tracks go. `mask.convert` writes a preset's outline as points; with shape keys, the points get keys at the union of their times (each the outline then) and the shape keys go.
- **Not yet:** the Mask tab, the canvas overlay, the engine.

| ---------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `mask.add`       | `mask_add`        | `clipIds`, exactly one of `preset` or `points` (3+), `values`, `mode`, `name` (default `Mask N`). Sizes default to half the frame. `clip_not_found`, `not_picture`, `invalid`. Returns `maskIds` by clip                                                                                                           |
| `mask.update`    | `mask_update`     | `clipId`, `maskId`, `patch` (any field but `id` and the points; numbers of `shape`), `at` for keyed fields (a key is written there). `clip_not_found`, `not_found`, `invalid`, `animated`                                                                                                                          |
| `mask.remove`    | `mask_remove`     | `clipId`, `maskIds` (empty: all) with their keys. `clip_not_found`, `not_found`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `mask.reorder`   | `mask_reorder`    | `clipId`, `maskId`, `index`. `not_found`, `invalid`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `mask.setPoints` | `mask_set_points` | `clipId`, `maskId`, `points` (3+, `{ id?, x, y, inX, inY, outX, outY }`; no id: new; unlisted: removed with keys), `insertAt` `{ after, t }` (the one new point, inserted on an animated path keeping each keyed shape), `at` (keys changed points of an animated path). `invalid`, `animated`. Returns `pointIds` |
| `mask.convert`   | `mask_convert`    | `clipId`, `maskId`: a rectangle, ellipse, star or heart becomes a path (scale 1, rotation stays on the mask), shape keys become point keys. `invalid` (linear, mirror, a path, a star whose point count is keyed across a change). Returns `pointIds`                                                              |
| `mask.setMatte`  | `mask_set_matte`  | `clipIds`, `trackId` or `null`, `by` (`alpha` default, `luma`), `invert`. `track_not_found`, `not_video`, `own_track`, `matte_chain`                                                                                                                                                                               |
| (read)           | `mask_list`       | `{ masks, matte }`: masks in order with their values at `at` (default your playhead), path points as `{ id, x, y, inX, inY, outX, outY }`                                                                                                                                                                          |
