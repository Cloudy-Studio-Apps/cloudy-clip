# mask

Masks on picture clips and track mattes. A mask is a shape (rectangle, ellipse, star, heart, linear, mirror, or a drawn path) in the clip's picture that decides where it shows, feathered, grown or shrunk, combined with the other masks by mode. A track matte shows a clip only where another video track's picture is. Like After Effects' masks and track mattes.

- **Tier:** feature
- **Requires:** `project` (the document, `orderedMasks`, `animatedClip`, `writeKey`, `maskOutline`, `insertPoint`), `element` (which kinds draw a picture), `preview` (the actor's playhead, the default `at` of `mask_list`, `previewProps`, `previewOverlays`), `keyframe` (`keyTargets`, `KeyAction`), `inspector` (the tab, sections, `FieldRow`, the open tab, `useClipAtPlayhead`), `timeline` (the clip selection), `transform` (`useVisibleLayers`, for a preset's default size and the canvas mapping; `hideBox`), `path` (`startPathEdit`, `startFreehand`, `startPenPoint`, `paintPath`, `framePoint`: the canvas gestures)
- **Contributes:** inspector tab `mask` (`inspectorTabs`, order 150, after Video) with sections `mask.section` (order 0: presets and None, Pen/Freehand set `ui.drawing`, the mask list, the selected mask's fields) and `mask.matte` (order 100: track select, Alpha/Brightness, Invert; always sends both `by` and `invert`) in `inspectorSections`, for clips on video tracks; key target `mask.keys` (`keyTargets`, `mask.keys.ts`): every keyable number of a video clip's masks, one group per mask (`prefix` `masks.<id>.`, the mask's name); point fields are labeled "Point <n> · X" (n from 1, in order). When the last key goes, `commitStatic` writes the value through `mask.update` (or `mask.setPoints` for a point); overlays `mask.overlay` and `mask.drawing` (`previewOverlays`); track control `mask.matteBadge` (`timelineTrackControls`, `ui/MatteBadge.tsx`): a mark on the header of a video track that is a matte (`matteTracks`), with the tooltip "Matte for: <clip names>" (names as the element's `display` labels them), nothing otherwise
- **Points it owns:** —
- **API** (`useModule(maskRef)`): `ui` (`MaskUiState`, per kernel, never in history: `selectedMaskId`, `selectedPointId`, `drawing`)
- **Files:**
  - `mask.commands.ts`: add, update, remove, reorder. `mask.matte.commands.ts`: `setMatte`. `mask.build.ts`: a new mask with the defaults. `mask.guards.ts`: `requirePictureClip`, `requireMask`, `checkRange`. `mask.schema.ts`: the Zod inputs.
  - `mask.points.commands.ts`: `setPoints`, `convert`. `logic/keys.ts`: `pathKeyTimes` (content times of a path's point and scale keys), sampling points and preset shapes at a content time, a preset's outline as points (`outlinePoints`, segments of no length at every time dropped by `dropFlat`), `keyInto`.
  - `mask.mcp.ts`: the tools, and `mask_list`. `mask.store.ts`: `createMaskUi()`.
- **Data:** `clip.masks`, `clip.maskOrder` and `clip.matte` live in the document (`project` declares and validates them, see its README). Mask space is output px from the picture's center, the picture fitted in the frame before the clip's transform. Key paths (all shared numbers, with `MASK_RANGES`): `masks.<id>.opacity | feather | expansion | x | y | rotation`, `masks.<id>.shape.width | height | roundness | points | inner | scale` (those its kind has) and per point `masks.<id>.shape.points.<pid>.x | y | inX | inY | outX | outY` (relative to the mask's x/y, before `scale`).
- **Invariants:** only picture clips on video tracks have masks or a matte. Ids from input (clips, masks, points, matte tracks) match own keys only (`Object.hasOwn`), so `constructor` or `__proto__` is `not_found`, never an existing record. Removing a mask drops its keys; removing the last drops `masks` and `maskOrder`. A matte names another video track of the clip's composition; a matte track has no matte of its own, and a clip on a matte track has none (`mask.matte_chain`). Every command is one undo step.
- **Animated paths:** when any point coordinate has keys, `mask.setPoints` with `at` keys the six coordinates of each changed point there (other points untouched); a point inserted with `insertAt` gets keys at every `pathKeyTimes` time, placed by `insertPoint` (de Casteljau) on that time's shape at the same `t`, and its neighbors' facing handles are keyed to match, so every keyed shape stays the same. A removed point's tracks go. `mask.convert` writes a preset's outline as points; with shape keys, the points get keys at the union of their times (each the outline then) and the shape keys go.
- **The tab** (`ui/`): `MaskPresets` (one `mask.add` to every selected picture clip, half the layer's fitted size; None runs `mask.remove` with no ids on each), `MaskRow` (color, name renamed by double-click, mode, invert, on, menu: convert, move, delete; a click sets `ui.selectedMaskId`), `MaskFields` + `MaskFieldRow` (rows from `logic/fields.ts` by kind, each with `KeyAction`; a drag draws through `previewProps`, release sends one `mask.update` at the playhead), `MatteSection`; `MatteBadge` is the track header's mark. `mask.edit.hook.ts` runs commands with failures as toasts. With several clips selected, only presets and the matte show.
- **On the canvas** (`ui/`, DOM only, never in a capture or export): every mapping goes through `logic/mapping.ts`: `frameMapping` is the clip's `maskPlacement` (from its visible layer's source and transform, a path's shift included, the same the engine uses) centered on the frame, and `toFrame` / `toMask` (`vecToFrame` / `vecToMask` for handles, `pointsToFrame` / `pointsToMask` for a path mask's points relative to its x/y before `scale`) carry points both ways. The gestures work in frame px; every result is mapped back into mask space before a command.
  - `MaskOverlay.tsx`: shown when exactly one clip on a video track is selected (a linked picture + sound counts as one, `mask.subject.hook.ts`), its picture is on screen, and the Mask tab is open or one of its masks is selected (`ui.selectedMaskId`); hidden while drawing. An SVG in the frame's own space (`viewBox` centered): every mask at the playhead outlined in its color (`MARKER_HEX`, `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`, dashed when off, `mask.paint.ts` `outlineData`: Béziers mapped exactly, linear and mirror as lines across the frame); a press on one selects it. While a mask is selected the transform box hides (`hideBox("mask")`) and the overlay takes the pointer: Escape or a press on empty space deselects it. A mask stays selected only while its clip is the one selected.
  - `MaskBox.tsx`, `mask.box.gesture.ts`, `logic/handles.ts`: the selected preset. Drag inside to move; corners scale (Shift keeps proportions, Alt from the center), sides stretch one axis, the top handle rotates (Shift: 15° steps), a double arrow below the bottom edge drags the feather, a dot inside the top-right corner the roundness (rectangle), a dot on the first inner corner `inner` (star). Linear: a center dot moves it, the handle on the hidden side rotates, the arrow on the shown side feathers; Mirror also has its two edges for the width. Gaps are in output px. The pointer is mapped into mask space (`presetHandles`, `dragPatch`, in the mask's own axes), so a handle stays under it however the clip is scaled, turned, flipped or carried by a path. A drag redraws the overlay in the DOM (`paintMask`) and the picture through `previewProps`; release sends one `mask.update` with `at` = the playhead; Escape cancels; a failure is a toast and the overlay goes back.
  - `MaskPathPoints.tsx`: the selected path mask, edited as the motion path is: `startPathEdit` on its points mapped into the frame (drag a point or a handle, Alt breaks the pair, the overlay redrawn by `paintPath`), release sends one `mask.setPoints` with every point's id and `at`; double-click within 24 output px of the line sends `mask.setPoints` with the split points and `insertAt` (`nearestOnPath`, `insertPoint`), the new point selected; Delete or Backspace (capture phase, not in text fields) removes the selected point while four or more stay; a drag inside moves the mask's x/y (`mask.update`, drawn live).
  - `MaskDrawing.tsx`: while `ui.drawing` (the tab's Pen / Freehand), over the frame with a small bar (Freehand / Pen, Done, Cancel), through `startFreehand` and `startPenPoint`. Freehand closes on release (the stroke fitted by `fitStroke` within 4 screen px); the pen closes on a click within 8 screen px of the first point, Enter, a double-click or Done. Three or more points send one `mask.add` with `points` mapped into mask space, then the new mask is selected; fewer send nothing. Escape or Cancel stops. Without the clip's picture on screen, drawing stops with a toast.
- **Not yet:** a path point drag draws only the overlay, not the picture (`startPathEdit` has no move callback).
